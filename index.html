JuntoCash

Split bills and budget, by Teko Labs

Split expenses with friends. Your share counts in your own budget.

JuntoCash keeps the expenses you share and the money you spend yourself in one app. When you split a dinner, your share counts in your own budget, under the category you choose. What you owe, what you’re owed and what’s left this month are on one screen.

With friends

Groups for a trip, a flat or a night out. Split equally, by amounts or by percentages. See who owes whom and settle up, all at once or part of it. Invite friends with a link.

On your own

Log what you spent, earned and saved. Set a monthly limit for everything or for one category, and see where the month went by category, on a calendar, or in a list you can search.

Private by design

JuntoCash works with no account, and then your expenses stay on your phone. With an account your data syncs between your phones, end-to-end encrypted: we can’t read your expenses, your groups or the names in them. There is no bank connection and there are no ads. The whole of it is in the privacy policy.

Where to get it

JuntoCash is an app for Android phones, in English and Portuguese. It is being tested now, and is free.
